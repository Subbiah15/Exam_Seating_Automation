The system uses an intelligent constraint-based optimization algorithm:

Key Features:
  • Subject Adjacency Prevention: Students studying the same subject are never seated adjacent (left, right, above, or below)
  • Department Row Separation: Students from the same department cannot sit in the same row
  • Hall Utilization: Optimizes seating to achieve 85-95% space utilization
  • Randomization: Uses randomization to prevent predictable patterns and reduce cheating opportunities
Benefits:
  • Extremely fast (< 1 second for 200+ students)
  • Prevents malpractice by strategic separation
  • Fair and transparent process
  • Fully auditable with complete logging

Hard Constraints (Always enforced):
  • Same-subject students cannot sit adjacent (horizontally or vertically)
  • Same-department students cannot sit in the same row
Soft Constraints (Optimization goals):
  • Maximize hall utilization
  • Minimize empty seats
  • Fair distribution across halls

File Upload Fails:

Check that your file has the correct format (reg_no, name, department, subject_code)

Generation Fails:

Ensure you have uploaded students and added at least one exam hall

Not Enough Seats:

The system will arrange as many students as possible. Add more halls if needed.
